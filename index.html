Research

I work on numerical methods and analysis for partial differential equations, with applications in computational science. My current focus is on structure-preserving finite element methods for problems in which the geometry itself moves — geometric flows, free boundaries, and fluid–structure interaction.

  • Parametric finite element methods for mean curvature flow, surface diffusion, and surface evolution with moving contact lines.
  • Arbitrary Lagrangian–Eulerian (ALE) finite element methods for fluid–structure interaction and free–boundary fluid dynamics.
  • Artificial tangential motion and minimal-deformation-rate techniques for mesh quality in long-time simulations.
  • ALE evolving finite element methods for shape optimization.
  • Structure-preserving discretizations for nonlinear Schrödinger equations and geometric flows.
